# Asymmetric Move

Find a smaller move at a bottleneck, default or interface.

## Steps

1. Map the flow, its bottlenecks, defaults and expensive dependencies.
2. Look for reuse, a bypass or a different dimension of value.
3. Preserve the requested outcome and expose permissions that still need checking.
4. Describe the smallest reversible move and its likely second-order effects.

## Use it when

The standard route rewards scale or resources we do not have.

## Leave it out when

The shortcut removes an essential safeguard or relies on deception.

# Testable Dissent

Turn disagreement into a discriminating observation.

## Steps

1. State the thesis precisely and write its strongest reasonable objection.
2. Separate discomfort about dissent from the quality of the evidence.
3. Identify observations supporting and weakening each account.
4. Design a reversible test whose outcomes distinguish the accounts.

## Use it when

A thesis has consensus but little exposure to a strong objection.

## Leave it out when

The disagreement is about values that an experiment cannot settle.

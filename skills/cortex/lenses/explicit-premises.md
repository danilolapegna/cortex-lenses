# Explicit Premises

Develop counterfactual alternatives while keeping binding choices explicit.

## Steps

1. Separate verified facts, binding decisions, preferences, inherited assumptions and unknowns.
2. Choose a nonessential premise and state the strongest reason to question it.
3. Describe the alternative without that premise and its transition cost.
4. Name the permission or evidence needed before the alternative becomes executable.

## Use it when

A stated preference may be hiding a materially better route.

## Leave it out when

The user has already settled the premise with awareness of its cost.

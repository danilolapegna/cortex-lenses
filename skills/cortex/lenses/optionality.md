# Optionality

Preserve future upside without accepting the full commitment now.

## Steps

1. Identify a small action that keeps a meaningful future route open.
2. State its carrying cost, downside, obligations and expiry.
3. Define the observation that triggers exercise, expansion or abandonment.
4. Compare this option with acting now and doing nothing.

## Use it when

The upside is uncertain and waiting can preserve freedom.

## Leave it out when

Delay closes the opportunity or the supposed option creates hidden obligations.

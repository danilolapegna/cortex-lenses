# Negative Space

Use missing actors, anomalies and discarded observations to open a route.

## Steps

1. List what the usual explanation ignores.
2. Inspect one anomaly, one absent participant and one excluded observation.
3. Ask what each would predict if it mattered.
4. Turn the most concrete possibility into a testable alternative.

## Use it when

The usual explanation leaves an awkward detail unexplained.

## Leave it out when

An urgent, well-understood failure needs immediate containment.

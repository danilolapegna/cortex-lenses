# Incentives Beat Advice

Explain and redesign behavior through incentives rather than stated intentions.

## Steps

1. Map who decides, knows, benefits and bears the costs.
2. Explain current behavior through rewards, risk, effort and delay.
3. Change one incentive, information flow or decision right.
4. Predict gaming and substitution, then propose an observable test.

## Use it when

Advice repeatedly fails to change behavior.

## Leave it out when

The actor lacks capability rather than motivation.

# Reverse a Disadvantage

Turn a constraint into a mechanism that a better-resourced rival cannot easily copy.

## Steps

1. Name the disadvantage and the evidence that it matters.
2. Look for resources it creates: focus, proximity, slack, urgency or unusual information.
3. Connect one resource to a fragile part of the problem.
4. Describe the reversal and a test that separates a real benefit from a comforting story.

## Use it when

A persistent constraint cannot cheaply be removed.

## Leave it out when

The constraint causes immediate harm that needs containment first.

# Step Inside

Use a temporary first-person viewpoint to expose pressures hidden from outside.

## Steps

1. Choose an element or participant to inhabit.
2. Describe what reaches it, what it can do and what constrains it.
3. Simulate a single journey through its interactions.
4. Translate a surprising pressure into a testable design requirement.
5. Mark imagined feelings as metaphor and verify real behavior separately.

## Use it when

An outside view obscures local friction or information.

## Leave it out when

Imagined experience would replace direct evidence about a person.

# Opponent Response

Expose a strategy to the strongest plausible response from an affected actor.

## Steps

1. Identify who loses from the proposal and what they can actually change.
2. Model their incentives, information, constraints and alternatives.
3. Write their strongest feasible response rather than a convenient mistake.
4. Develop a counter-move and an early signal that would distinguish the responses.

## Use it when

A competitor, counterparty or gatekeeper can adapt after we act.

## Leave it out when

The system is passive and no actor can change the outcome.

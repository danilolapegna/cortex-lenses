# Structural Analogy

Transfer a causal pattern rather than the appearance of another solution.

## Steps

1. Map the target functions, relationships, constraints and failure modes.
2. Find a distant domain with a matching structure.
3. Extract the mechanism and map each essential relationship to the target.
4. State where the analogy breaks and how to test the transfer.

## Use it when

The problem has recognizable dynamics beyond its own industry.

## Leave it out when

The resemblance is visual or verbal and has no causal mapping.

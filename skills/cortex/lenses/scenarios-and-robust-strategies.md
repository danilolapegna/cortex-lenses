# Scenarios and Robust Strategies

Choose a bounded strategy and contingent responses across distinct futures.

## Steps

1. Identify decision-changing uncertainties and verified anchors.
2. Separate what can be predicted from what can be influenced.
3. Build materially different plausible scenarios without invented probabilities.
4. Compare moves; attach a signpost, response, owner and exposure cap to contingent actions.
5. State the horizon and observation that invalidates the strategy.

## Use it when

The same commitment faces several plausible futures.

## Leave it out when

One missing fact can close the choice directly.

# Ladder of Whys

Move from the requested implementation to the outcome it serves.

## Steps

1. Write the requested outcome without naming a tool.
2. Ask why it matters two or three times, stopping before the answer becomes generic.
3. For each intermediate goal, find a different way to achieve it.
4. Bring those routes back to the original constraints.

## Use it when

The requested solution seems narrower than the need.

## Leave it out when

The objective is already clear and the remaining question is factual.

# Uncomfortable Routes

Expose overlooked mechanisms without disguising their consequences.

## Steps

1. Name a relevant route excluded by comfortable framing.
2. Explain the concern about trust, relationships, rights or reputation.
3. Find a clean variant that preserves its useful mechanism.
4. Keep conditions explicit; exclude fraud, deception, misuse of personal data and harm.

## Use it when

Social discomfort is hiding a legitimate alternative.

## Leave it out when

The route depends on wrongdoing or creates no useful alternative.

# Mutation Checklist

Generate variants by applying distinct operations to a concrete design.

## Steps

1. Choose the product or process and preserve its required function.
2. Try substitution, combination, adaptation, resizing and another use.
3. Try elimination, rearrangement and reversal.
4. Keep the variants separate until each has an intelligible mechanism.

## Use it when

There is a concrete design to transform.

## Leave it out when

The task is still too vague to identify what is being changed.

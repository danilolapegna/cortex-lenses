# Independent Difference

## What can go wrong

Many labels conceal one mechanism or a contaminated search.

## How to check

Inspect what each agent actually saw. Compare mechanisms, actors, timing and representations after generation; preserve originals and conditions even when grouping. Disclose missing isolation and cosmetic novelty.

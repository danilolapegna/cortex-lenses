# Causal Mechanism

## What can go wrong

An analogy or plausible narrative is mistaken for a working mechanism.

## How to check

Trace the intervention to the outcome through intermediate signals. Write a competing explanation and a test that distinguishes them. For analogies, inspect the transferred relationship and its breaking point.

# Downside and Reversibility

## What can go wrong

Attractive upside hides an irreversible loss or harm to others.

## How to check

Define the real downside and cap exposure. Check permissions, rights, privacy and collateral effects before action. Prefer a reversible probe and give a recovery path; selection alone is not authorization.
